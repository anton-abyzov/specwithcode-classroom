# Demo script (about 2 minutes)

**0:00 The problem.** "This is Maya, 7th grade in Miami. Her class runs out of free AI by third period. Her school already has AI credits, a cheap subscription, a free tier and a lab computer. They are just in four places."

**0:20 The Key Ring.** Point at the sidebar: four providers in the school's policy order, each with a daily budget. "OpenAI nonprofit credits first, then DeepSeek, then Gemini's free tier, then the library Mac."

**0:35 Failover, live.** Tap **Run out** on *OpenAI nonprofit grant*. Click the **Physics: projectile** chip. When the answer lands, point at the route trail: `OpenAI · HTTP 429` crossed out, then `DeepSeek ✓`. "The grant ran out mid-class. Maya never saw an error. The auto-switch counter went up and families paid $0."

**1:00 Real work: math.** Scroll the answer: steps in LaTeX, checked with SymPy, a "check yourself" question at the end. Flip **Learn / Finish**: "Teachers choose whether it finishes the work or leaves the last step."

**1:15 Real work: slides.** Tap **Refill** on OpenAI. Click **Slides on volcanoes**. Page through the deck, show speaker notes, click **Download .pptx**.

**1:35 Real work: an app.** Click **Build a book club app**. The phone frame shows a working app; add reading minutes and watch the progress ring move.

**1:50 Close.** "Built on the OpenAI Agents SDK and OpenAI API, with Codex. Any school can deploy it today and turn credits it already has into AI help that never runs out."
