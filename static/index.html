    What are we working on, Maya?

    Ask for slides, a worked math or physics problem, or a website or app you build yourself.

    Runs on the OpenAI Agents SDK and the OpenAI API, built with Codex. Made at the DevDay Exchange hackathon in Miami.

    How it works

    1. The school connects what it already has. OpenAI plans with GPT-6 Luna credits, a DeepSeek subscription, the Gemini free tier, and computers in the building running open models.
    2. A coach sends each request to a specialist. Slides, math and physics, or app building. App projects start with a short spec of what the app must do, then the code.
    3. Every step goes to the first one with tokens left. If it runs out or goes down mid-answer, the next one finishes the job. Students never see an error, and families pay nothing.

    Teachers choose Learn (hints, the student does the last step) or Finish (complete work, explained). Every request passes a K-12 safety check.
